## Writing

Everything starts as plain-text notes I keep for myself. A few of them become things here. I draft and edit with help from an LLM, including agent skills, and I decide what goes out.

## Dates and stages

A note’s date is when the idea first showed up in my notes, not when the page was written. Each notes also show a stage:

- **Seedling:** a rough first thought, recently planted. Expect gaps.
- **Budding:** taking shape, revised a few times, and still growing.
- **Evergreen:** settled enough to stand on its own, though still tended.

## Connections

Notes link to each other with a named verb, like `informs` or `part of`. Every verb and every connection is listed on [Relations](/rrelations).

## Setting

Text is set in Schibsted Grotesk. Paths, dates and labels are in IBM Plex Mono. Both are served from this site. The paper is #F4F5F2 and the ink is #15191C.

## The mark and the sketch

The NN mark is 32 small squares. Hover over it and they flicker, like a machine at work. The drawing on the home page is an untitled Processing sketch of mine in JavaScript from around 2017–18, redrawn in plain canvas in 2026.

## Building

Each note is a Markdown file. A small TypeScript build turns them into plain HTML and CSS, checks every link and connection, and publishes to GitHub Pages.
